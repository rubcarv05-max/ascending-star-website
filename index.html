Questions

Straight answers.

How long does it take?

Most websites go live in about 1 day. A full package (website, Google Business Profile and SEO setup) is usually ready in under a week. SEO results then build over the following weeks and months.

Is it expensive?

It depends on what you need, and you decide. On the free call we show you the options. If the price is too high, we take features out until it fits your budget. We'll also tell you when you don't need something, like expensive ads.

Is it worth it?

If people are searching for your service in your city and can't find you, they're calling someone else. A good website and Google presence work for you 24/7. And we'll be honest: if we don't think we can help your business, we'll say so on the call.

Should I do Google Ads or SEO?

Ads can bring calls quickly, but they get expensive and stop the moment you stop paying. SEO takes a little longer but keeps bringing customers in. For most small businesses we recommend starting with SEO.

I don't have a domain yet. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We buy and set up the domain for you. A .com usually costs more than options like .shop, and we'll explain the choices on the call.

Where do you work, and in what language?

We work with small businesses in Brazil, starting in Fortaleza, and in Florida, USA. Everything can be done in English or Portuguese.
